Stock & Crypto Backtests: Strategy vs. Buy and Hold

This dashboard compares its historical performance with buying and holding the same asset. Explore backtest charts, period-by-period return tables, and the latest recorded signals across stocks, gold, and cryptocurrencies.

Updates and Important Notes

The dashboard uses daily price data and is updated once per day. It is not a real-time service. Always check the data date shown on each asset page.

Returns shown in the period tables are cumulative, not annualized. Backtest results exclude transaction fees and slippage. Historical performance and AI commentary do not guarantee future returns.
